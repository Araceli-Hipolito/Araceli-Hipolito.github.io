About

Hi, I'm Araceli — a Senior Product Designer based in Guadalajara, Mexico.

I have 7+ years of experience designing B2B and B2C digital products across SaaS, FinTech, EdTech, gaming, PropTech, creator economy, and emerging industries.

My work spans the full product lifecycle, from discovery and product definition to research, interaction design, prototyping, UI, testing, and delivery.

I'm especially drawn to complex products where design can turn business rules, systems, and user needs into experiences that feel clear and intuitive.

Outside of product design, I'm drawn to reading, music, visual culture, games, my cat, and the details that make experiences memorable.
